A destination for your wellbeing.

Dental tourism brings together an individualized clinical plan and a stay away from home. At Implandent, Dr. Miguel Ángel Carela Gutiérrez and the team can assess your case, explain treatment phases, estimate appointments and coordinate timing before you travel.

The Dominican Republic attracts international patients for its trained dental professionals, the possibility of competitive costs and its world-renowned holiday setting. Every quote and treatment plan is personalized; travel and accommodation are arranged separately.

Meet Dr. Carela.

Oral implant specialist and CEO of Implandent since 2021.

Full biography

My name is Miguel Ángel Carela Gutiérrez. I graduated in dentistry from Universidad Central del Este on February 25, 2011.

I specialized in oral implantology at Centro Médico UCE and graduated in 2019.

During my academic training, I completed courses in oral bone grafting, implant-supported prosthetics and strategic corticobasal implantology, among others.

I have been CEO of Clínica Odontológica Especializada Implandent since 2021.

I am an active member of Colegio Dominicano de Odontólogos (CDO) and Sociedad Dominicana de Implantología Oral (SDIO).
