FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Answers that bring peace of mind.

If your situation is specific, get in touch to arrange an assessment.

How can I request an appointment?+

Call, email or start a WhatsApp conversation. The clinic confirms availability directly.

Where is Implandent located?+

Calle Francisco Castillo Márquez núm. 48, La Romana 22000, República Dominicana.

Which treatments does the clinic offer?+

Implants, prosthetics, periodontics, orthodontics, oral surgery, pediatric dentistry, aesthetic dentistry and endodontics are listed areas of care.

Do I need an assessment before treatment?+

Yes. An assessment helps identify your needs and discuss suitable options.

Do you see children?+

Pediatric dentistry is among the clinic’s listed care areas. Please ask about availability for a child’s visit.

Can I ask about treatment cost?+

Yes. Cost depends on the assessment and proposed plan, so prices are not listed without an evaluation.

What are your opening hours?+

Monday to Friday, 9:00–12:00 and 14:00–18:00; Saturdays, 9:00–12:00.
